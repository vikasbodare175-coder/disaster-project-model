Disaster Management Project
📌 About the Project

The Disaster Management Project aims to provide information about natural and human-made disasters, such as floods, earthquakes, cyclones, and fires. It helps people understand disaster risks, learn safety precautions, and prepare for emergencies.

🎯 Objectives
Create awareness about different types of disasters.
Explain the causes and effects of disasters.
Provide safety tips and preventive measures.
Help people understand emergency preparedness and response.
🌍 Types of Disasters
Natural Disasters: Earthquakes, floods, cyclones, droughts, and landslides.
Human-Made Disasters: Fires, industrial accidents, chemical leaks, and transportation accidents.
🛡️ Key Features
Information about different disasters.
Disaster prevention and safety guidelines.
Emergency preparedness tips.
Awareness about rescue and relief operations.
🚀 Benefits

This project helps improve disaster awareness, encourages preparedness, and promotes safety to reduce the impact of disasters on people and the environment.

📚 Conclusion

Disaster management plays an important role in protecting lives and property. Proper planning, awareness, and timely action can help reduce disaster-related risks and losses.

👨‍💻 Project Status

This project is developed for educational purposes to spread awareness about disaster management.
